The Story

The Heart of STL Blues

Torrey Casey is a dynamic frontman and vocalist known for delivering authentic, high-energy blues and soul. Whether he's hosting the legendary Twin City Fun Blues Jam or headlining with The Torrey Casey Band, Torrey brings an undeniable stage presence that captivates audiences.

With deep roots in the St. Louis music scene, The Torrey Casey Band blends traditional blues aesthetics with modern R&B grooves. Torrey is also a leading event entrepreneur, elevating private events with his signature Rotofoto 360 experience.
